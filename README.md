# Inside India's UPI Economy: A Business Analytics Case Study

## Project Overview

This project analyzes India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) ecosystem to understand market growth, competition between payment apps, and regional transaction patterns.

Using Python and exploratory data analysis, the project examines three datasets covering overall UPI statistics, app-wise transactions, and state-wise transactions.

## Business Questions

1. How is India's UPI market changing over time?
2. How do major UPI payment apps compare in transaction activity?
3. Which states and union territories have the highest UPI activity and growth?
4. What potential business opportunities can be identified from these trends?

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

## Datasets and Analysis

### 1. Overall UPI Market Analysis
- Analyzed monthly transaction volume and transaction value.
- Calculated monthly growth percentages.
- Examined average transaction amounts.
- Visualized changes in overall UPI activity.

### 2. App-wise Competition Analysis
- Compared PhonePe, Google Pay, and Paytm.
- Examined transaction volumes and growth.
- Calculated transaction shares within the three selected apps.
- Used common reporting months for a fair comparison.

### 3. State-wise UPI Analysis
- Analyzed 36 states and union territories.
- Identified top regions by transaction volume and value.
- Calculated average transaction amounts.
- Examined first-to-last-month transaction growth.
- Classified geographical markets using transaction volume and growth.

## Key Findings

- Maharashtra recorded the highest cumulative transaction volume and transaction value in the state-wise dataset.
- Telangana ranked fourth in transaction volume and third in transaction value.
- Lakshadweep recorded the highest first-to-last-month transaction growth percentage among the analyzed regions.
- PhonePe had the largest transaction share among the three selected apps in the common reporting period.
- Overall UPI transaction volume reached its highest level in August 2026 in the six-month dataset.

## Business Recommendations

- Strengthen merchant services and customer experience in established high-volume markets.
- Investigate emerging regions with high transaction growth.
- Monitor competitive positioning among major UPI apps.
- Study regional differences in average transaction amounts.
- Track transaction volume and value to understand changes in payment activity.

## Project Limitations

- Overall UPI statistics cover only April to September 2026.
- App-wise comparison includes only PhonePe, Google Pay, and Paytm.
- The app-wise comparison uses 31 common reporting months.
- State-wise growth compares April 2023 with September 2026 and is not CAGR.
- The analysis identifies patterns but does not establish the causes of regional differences.
